    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    What is BLUMI?
    BLUMI is an independent digital brand being built within the Base ecosystem. It focuses on creativity, community, technology and innovation, with the BLUMI mascot as its face. The plan is simple: brand first, ecosystem next.
    Has the BLUMI token launched?
    Is BLUMI affiliated with Base or Coinbase?
    No. BLUMI is an independent project building on Base. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Base or Coinbase.
    What does BLUMI stand for?
    Beyond Limits, United Minds, Innovation: the three principles behind everything we build.
    How can I join the community?
    Are the ecosystem products live?
    Not yet. Community, Playground, Collectibles and Labs are planned concepts that will be shaped together with the community. The mini game on this page is a small, just-for-fun preview of the Playground idea.
    How do I stay safe from scams?
    Only trust the links listed under Official Channels. BLUMI will never DM you first, never ask for your seed phrase or private key, and never ask you to send crypto to receive something back.
    Can I use the BLUMI mascot in my own content?
    Yes, for fan art, memes and community content. Keep the mascot's original shape, proportions and colours, and don't use it on unofficial tokens or to suggest something is official when it isn't.
    Official Channels

    Only trust these.

    Scammers often copy names and logos. These are the only official BLUMI channels. Bookmark this page and check here before you click, join or buy anything.

    BLUMI will never

    • DM you first about tokens, presales or airdrops
    • Ask for your seed phrase or private key
    • Ask you to send crypto to "verify" or receive rewards
    • Share a contract address only in DMs or replies
    • Ask you to connect your wallet on another site

    Spotted a fake? Let us know in the official Telegram so the community can stay safe.
